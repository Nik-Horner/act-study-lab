# English and Reading naturalness audit

The reported laboratory question had a valid key but forced the reader through “began disagreeing describes.” That is a sentence-design defect. The revised version marks the added clause with commas and leaves a straightforward main sentence: “The series of reports describes a fault in the comparison procedure.” Feedback now shows that sentence before explaining the other verbs.

The review screened the wording of 758 English items/checks and 364 active/held-out Reading items, including 56 complete Reading passages. It also checked the English worked examples and 106 skill activities, 42 Reading worked examples and 42 formative slots. The focus was natural language, understandable clause boundaries, unambiguous references, complete local context and explanations tied to the actual sentence. Intentionally incorrect editing targets remain incorrect until the right choice is inserted.

## Repairs

- Marked embedded relative clauses clearly and showed their underlying main sentences in drills, worked examples and try-its.
- Replaced an unsupported contrast between cloth trials with a real expectation-versus-result contrast.
- Replaced artificial internal commas in a complex list with natural descriptions that actually require clear item boundaries.
- Repaired awkward modifiers, noun/verb pairings and surrounding text outside the underline, including *loafs*, a violation “being” a fine, “press given by newspapers,” and an idea that “informs laboratories.”
- Removed ambiguous references to a radiator, a notebook/runner’s hand and people completing an orientation.
- Corrected the timing of beach measurements and made the before/after library counting periods explicit.
- Replaced opaque answer wording such as “useful engagement,” “effective explanation” and “accumulated making” with specific actions supported by the passage.
- Expanded three incomplete formative excerpts to their complete existing source passages, so references to an instruction, a technique or an earlier action have antecedents.
- Preserved every assessment ID and answer position. Two English correct phrases and seven newer Reading correct phrases were reworded without changing the intended answer or its position. Existing 643 historical Reading/Science answer-text contracts remain intact.

The companion JSON ledger records each before/after revision and the frozen pre-edit grading hash for all 1,122 assessment positions. Existing English question/skill and Reading source-evidence ledgers were refreshed for the actual revised content. New regressions check the reported sentence, full-span repairs, clear hand references, study timing, shared-passage consistency and complete formative contexts.

This is an editorial audit, not psychometric ACT score calibration. It does not classify every difficult sentence as defective: necessary technical language and meaningful clause complexity remain, with clearer explanations.
