# Adaptive lessons and Daily challenges — 2026-10-18

Daily lesson selection now distinguishes the easiest authored entry to a skill from its hardest extension. Section scores establish a supported starting level; the goal alone cannot move a beginner directly into hard work. Initial extra questions stay within the chosen level. Complete skill pools remain available for later independent review and recovery.

The planner uses the first recorded answer per canonical question, in chronological order. Two fresh successes at the current level unlock the next tier; a miss or low-confidence response restores support for that skill. Repeated IDs, demonstrated worked-example answers, and familiar Reading passages cannot manufacture promotion. This changes instructional support, not a stored ACT score, mastery credit, or completed curriculum unit. Campaign stars remain separate.

Foundation teaching keeps the interactive exploration, paginated explanations, and numbered worked reasoning. Core/Challenge pages that contain only prerequisite rules provide a compact complete refresher, without replaying the beginner interaction. Actual source evidence, examples, guided checks, and independent questions remain intact. No prerequisite text or saved checkpoint is removed.

Lessons now expose their real Learn/Explore/Practice rounds with earned completion marks. Guided recovery ends with “Challenge cleared,” then independent practice. The Daily completion screen shows the existing 100-XP session reward and level; revisiting cannot earn another reward. No new reward system, settings, dependency, or database migration.
