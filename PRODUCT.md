# ACT Study Lab

A student preparing for the enhanced digital ACT can study English, Math, Reading, and Science from one website and one existing Supabase account. English Lab is the incumbent product; this work extends its routes, account model, and visual identity rather than replacing them.

The immediate user is retaking the ACT on October 17, aiming for 36 in Math and Science, after a 34 Math and 27 Science. Available study time is approximately 60–90 minutes per day. Those are the user's goals, not defaults to assign to every account. Profiles supply each student's target and test date. New-subject baseline scores are unset unless actually supported by stored account data.

The primary scene is an evening study session on a computer: identify today's focused work, study a lesson, answer questions with evidence, review mistakes, and rehearse a timed section. Phone layouts support studying and review; full screen-based test rehearsal is best practiced on a computer and with ACT's official interface.

Success means the existing sign-in and English progress keep working, added subjects are findable from the dashboard, completed work persists through the existing database, original Reading content has substantive lessons and full-length passages, and missed tasks remain visible within the preparation plan's budget.

Truth boundaries: original questions are independent practice; their raw accuracy is not a validated official scaled-score prediction. Science is optional under the enhanced ACT. Active timers are device-scoped with fixed deadlines; completed history is account-scoped. The daily budget preference is explicitly browser-scoped. Math + Science and Reading are distinct focus plans rather than simultaneous extra daily workloads. No Canvas artifacts.
